# Warranty Vault User Help Guide

Warranty Vault helps you keep product warranty details and proof documents in one place. Use it for invoices, warranty cards, manuals, product photos, service contacts, and expiry tracking.

## What You Can Track

For each product, you can save:

- Product name
- Brand
- Category
- Purchase date
- Warranty end date
- Invoice amount in rupees
- Purchase source: online or offline
- Store, platform, or seller name
- Store address, when applicable
- Support or point-of-contact details
- Notes
- Supporting documents such as invoices, bills, warranty cards, manuals, PDFs, and images

## Dashboard Overview

The top dashboard shows quick numbers:

- `Tracked items` - total products saved
- `Active warranties` - products still under warranty
- `Expiring soon` - products nearing the end of warranty
- `Invoice value` - total saved invoice value

Use these numbers to quickly understand your current coverage.

## Warranty Portfolio

The Warranty Portfolio shows your saved products as cards.

Each card shows:

- Product name
- Brand and category
- Warranty status
- Remaining warranty period
- Warranty end date
- Invoice value
- Whether proof documents are saved

The colored coverage bar gives a quick visual sense of warranty health:

- Green means the warranty is active
- Amber means it is expiring soon
- Red means it has expired

Click a product card to view full details.

## Filter Products

Use the status filters above the portfolio:

- `All`
- `Active`
- `Expiring`
- `Expired`

These help you focus on products that need attention.

## View Product Details

When you select a product, the detail panel shows:

- Purchase date
- Remaining warranty
- Invoice amount
- Purchase source
- Supporting documents
- Store or platform details
- Contact details
- Notes

Documents are shown before long notes so you can find proof files quickly.

## Add A Product

Select `Add Product` and fill in the product details.

Recommended minimum details:

- Product name
- Purchase date
- Warranty end date
- Invoice amount, if known
- Store or platform
- At least one supporting document

You can save the item after entering the required fields.

## Add Supporting Documents

When adding or editing a product, attach useful proof files such as:

- Invoice or bill
- Warranty card
- Product registration confirmation
- Manual
- Service plan document
- Product image

Supported files can include images and PDFs.

## Preview Or Download Documents

In the selected product detail panel, open the `Documents` section.

Use the document actions to:

- Preview a saved document
- Download the document for future use

This is useful when you need to claim warranty service or share proof of purchase.

## Edit A Product

Select a product, then use the edit button.

You can update:

- Product details
- Warranty dates
- Invoice amount
- Store/platform information
- Contact details
- Notes
- Supporting documents

Existing documents remain attached unless removed by a future delete feature.

## Use Notes Well

Use notes for extra information such as:

- Serial number
- Registration number
- Warranty terms summary
- Claim instructions
- Exclusions
- Customer support history

Long notes are collapsed in the detail panel. Use `Show more` to read the full note.

## Good Habits

- Add the invoice or bill as soon as you buy a product.
- Add the warranty card or registration proof if available.
- Record the serial number in notes.
- Add the support phone, email, or website in contact details.
- Check `Expiring soon` occasionally so you do not miss claim windows.

## Privacy Note

Warranty Vault is designed for your personal warranty records. Avoid uploading documents you do not need, and review invoices or files before saving them if they contain extra sensitive information.
